Who We Are

A registered social services organization committed to uplifting underprivileged children and families in Bhayandar East.

Helping Hands, Changing Lives

Anshika Helping Hands Foundation is a grassroots NGO based in Narmada Nagar, Bhayandar East. We focus on child welfare, education, food distribution, healthcare, and environment.

Our team of dedicated volunteers works tirelessly to provide food, education, and basic necessities to those who need it most.

How We Work

A simple, transparent process.

1. Identify

Volunteers identify children in need through schools.

2. Verify

We verify each family's situation with home visits.

3. Support

Fees, books, uniforms provided directly.

4. Follow-Up

We track each child's progress regularly.
